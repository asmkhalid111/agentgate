## 3. Explicit Summary of Decisions Submitted for Final Approval

The following specific architectural decisions are submitted for final reviewer ratification:

1. **Official MCP Starter Coordinates & Protocol Handling:** Ratification of `spring-ai-starter-mcp-server-webmvc` and `spring-ai-starter-mcp-client` via `spring-ai-bom:2.0.2`, Streamable HTTP configured on `/mcp`, and verification of negotiated protocol versions in M01.
2. **Write-Tool Idempotency Model:** Ratification of explicit client `operationId`, two-phase intent/outcome recording, and prohibition of blind retries on unknown downstream status.
3. **Authoritative 5-State Approval Machine & Disconnection Retention:** Ratification of the single transition table (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`), and the rule that client disconnection does not cancel approval.
4. **Pre-Dispatch Authorization & Limit Reservation:** Ratification of the Step 8a synchronous pre-dispatch assertion and two-phase limit reservation (preventing double-charging).
5. **`azp`-Bound User Delegation:** Ratification of the authorized-party verification protocol for `X-User-Assertion` tokens.
6. **Crash-Safe Audit WAL Framing:** Ratification of binary framing with CRC32 checksums, persistent volume mounting, and continuous reconciliation ordering.
7. **Narrow M01 Boundary & Approval to Implement:** Formal verdict of **`APPROVED`** to commence Milestone M01 skeleton setup.
