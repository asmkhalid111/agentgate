# Prepare reviewer handoff for milestone {{MILESTONE_ID}}

Read the approved milestone plan, full Git diff, `COMPLETION.md`, current tests and requirement matrix. Produce a concise package for external reviewer:
1. Approved scope and acceptance requirements.
2. Files changed grouped by business module.
3. Commit hashes and commands actually run.
4. Exact tests passed/failed/skipped with evidence paths and limitations.
5. Security/edge cases; any known bypass or unresolved weakness.
6. Screenshots or example request-response transcripts of running workflows where useful.
7. Updated requirement statuses plus proposed next milestone.
Do not claim verification on the basis of your own narrative alone. Do not proceed automatically.
